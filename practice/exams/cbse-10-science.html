Electricity

Q1. The SI unit of electric resistance is the:

  1. Volt
  2. Ampere
  3. Ohm
  4. Watt
Show answer

C. Ohm — Resistance is measured in ohms (Ω). The volt is potential difference, the ampere is current, and the watt is power.

Chemical Reactions

Q2. The common name of sodium hydrogencarbonate (NaHCO₃) is:

  1. Washing soda
  2. Baking soda
  3. Common salt
  4. Bleaching powder
Show answer

B. Baking soda — NaHCO₃ is baking soda (sodium bicarbonate). Washing soda is Na₂CO₃·10H₂O, common salt is NaCl, and bleaching powder is CaOCl₂.

Human Eye

Q3. The coloured part of the human eye that controls the size of the pupil is the:

  1. Cornea
  2. Retina
  3. Iris
  4. Lens
Show answer

C. Iris — The iris is the coloured, muscular diaphragm that adjusts the size of the pupil to control how much light enters. The retina is the light-sensitive screen at the back of the eye.

Life Processes

Q4. The process by which green plants prepare their own food using sunlight is called:

  1. Respiration
  2. Transpiration
  3. Photosynthesis
  4. Digestion
Show answer

C. Photosynthesis — Photosynthesis uses sunlight, carbon dioxide and water in the chlorophyll of leaves to make glucose and release oxygen. Respiration is the reverse process that releases energy.

Human Eye

Q5. The image formed on the retina of a normal human eye is:

  1. Real and inverted
  2. Virtual and erect
  3. Real and erect
  4. Virtual and inverted
Show answer

A. Real and inverted — The convex lens of the eye forms a real, inverted, diminished image on the retina. The brain interprets it as upright.

Life Processes

Q6. During respiration, glucose is broken down mainly to release:

  1. Chlorophyll
  2. Energy
  3. Proteins
  4. Sunlight
Show answer

B. Energy — Respiration breaks down glucose (usually with oxygen) to release energy stored as ATP, along with carbon dioxide and water.

Chemical Reactions And Equations

Q7. A chemical reaction in which a single reactant breaks down into two or more simpler products is best classified as a:

  1. Combination reaction
  2. Decomposition reaction
  3. Displacement reaction
  4. Double displacement reaction
Show answer

B. Decomposition reaction — In a decomposition reaction one compound splits into simpler substances, often on heating, exposure to light, or passing electricity.

Acids Bases And Salts

Q8. Which of the following solutions has a pH value greater than 7?

  1. Lemon juice
  2. Vinegar
  3. Milk of magnesia
  4. Gastric juice
Show answer

C. Milk of magnesia — Milk of magnesia is a suspension of magnesium hydroxide, a base, so its pH is above 7; the other three are acidic (pH below 7).

Metals And Non Metals

Q9. Which metal, when added to an aqueous solution of copper sulphate, will displace copper from it?

  1. Silver
  2. Gold
  3. Mercury
  4. Iron
Show answer

D. Iron — Iron lies above copper in the reactivity series, so it is more reactive and displaces copper; silver, gold and mercury are less reactive than copper.

Carbon And Its Compounds

Q10. The functional group present in ethanoic acid (acetic acid) is the:

  1. Hydroxyl group (-OH)
  2. Aldehyde group (-CHO)
  3. Carboxylic acid group (-COOH)
  4. Ketone group (>C=O)
Show answer

C. Carboxylic acid group (-COOH) — Ethanoic acid, CH3COOH, contains the -COOH carboxylic acid functional group, which gives it its acidic character.

Chemical Reactions And Equations

Q11. Which of the following is an endothermic reaction?

  1. Burning of natural gas
  2. Respiration in living cells
  3. Neutralisation of an acid by a base
  4. Thermal decomposition of calcium carbonate
Show answer

D. Thermal decomposition of calcium carbonate — Heating calcium carbonate to form calcium oxide and carbon dioxide absorbs heat, making it endothermic; combustion, respiration and neutralisation all release heat.

Life Processes

Q12. In the human respiratory system, the exchange of gases between the blood and the air takes place in the:

  1. Alveoli
  2. Bronchi
  3. Trachea
  4. Larynx
Show answer

A. Alveoli — The alveoli are thin-walled, balloon-like air sacs with a rich blood supply, providing a large surface for oxygen and carbon dioxide exchange.

Control And Coordination

Q13. Which endocrine gland secretes the hormone insulin?

  1. Thyroid
  2. Pancreas
  3. Adrenal
  4. Pituitary
Show answer

B. Pancreas — Insulin is produced by the pancreas and lowers blood glucose; its deficiency causes diabetes.

Control And Coordination

Q14. Reflex actions in the human body, such as pulling the hand away from a hot object, are controlled mainly by the:

  1. Cerebrum
  2. Cerebellum
  3. Spinal cord
  4. Medulla
Show answer

C. Spinal cord — Reflex arcs are completed through the spinal cord, allowing a rapid response without waiting for the brain to process the signal.

How Do Organisms Reproduce

Q15. Which of the following organisms reproduces asexually by budding?

  1. Amoeba
  2. Planaria
  3. Hydra
  4. Plasmodium
Show answer

C. Hydra — Hydra reproduces by budding, where a small outgrowth develops into a new individual; Amoeba uses binary fission, Plasmodium multiple fission, and Planaria regeneration.

Heredity

Q16. When two pea plants heterozygous for height (Tt) are crossed, the ratio of tall to dwarf plants obtained in the offspring is:

  1. 3 : 1
  2. 1 : 1
  3. 1 : 2 : 1
  4. 9 : 3 : 3 : 1
Show answer

A. 3 : 1 — A Tt x Tt cross gives genotypes 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt, but since T is dominant the visible (phenotypic) ratio of tall to dwarf is 3 : 1.

Heredity And Evolution

Q17. The forelimb of a human and the wing of a bird have a similar basic structure but perform different functions. Such organs are called:

  1. Analogous organs
  2. Homologous organs
  3. Vestigial organs
  4. Acquired characters
Show answer

B. Homologous organs — Homologous organs share the same fundamental structure and origin but may be adapted for different functions, indicating common ancestry.

Human Eye And Colourful World

Q18. A person who cannot see distant objects clearly suffers from myopia. This defect is corrected by using spectacles with a:

  1. Concave lens
  2. Convex lens
  3. Cylindrical lens
  4. Plano-convex lens
Show answer

A. Concave lens — Myopia is corrected with a concave (diverging) lens of suitable power, which moves the image back onto the retina.

Light Reflection And Refraction

Q19. The power of a convex lens whose focal length is 50 cm is:

  1. +0.5 D
  2. +5 D
  3. +50 D
  4. +2 D
Show answer

D. +2 D — Power = 1/f (in metres) = 1/0.5 = +2 dioptres for a converging lens.

Electricity

Q20. Two resistors of 2 ohm and 3 ohm are connected in series. The equivalent resistance of the combination is:

  1. 1.2 ohm
  2. 5 ohm
  3. 6 ohm
  4. 0.6 ohm
Show answer

B. 5 ohm — In series, resistances add up: 2 + 3 = 5 ohm. (1.2 ohm would be the parallel combination.)

Magnetic Effects Of Electric Current

Q21. The magnetic field lines inside a long current-carrying solenoid are:

  1. Circular
  2. Radially outward
  3. Concentric circles
  4. Parallel straight lines
Show answer

D. Parallel straight lines — Inside a solenoid the magnetic field is nearly uniform, shown by parallel straight field lines, similar to the field of a bar magnet outside it.

Our Environment

Q22. In a food chain, the approximate percentage of energy transferred from one trophic level to the next is:

  1. 1%
  2. 100%
  3. 10%
  4. 50%
Show answer

C. 10% — According to the ten percent law, only about 10% of the energy at one trophic level is passed on to the next; the rest is lost mainly as heat.
