From classroom to workforce

Skills United Arab Emirates's leading export sectors draw on.

  • Services (40% of exports): reading, writing and speaking well, numbers, digital skills, working with people
  • Minerals and energy (33% of exports): earth science, chemistry, engineering, safety
  • Precious stones and metals (14% of exports): careful measuring, craft, trade and finance

Every one of these starts with the same foundations: number sense, reading and sounds, and curiosity about the world. That is where EC Play starts, free, with Grade 1.
