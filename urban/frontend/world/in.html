Rule used: "builds talent the world employs" means net migration is negative and remittances are at least 3% of GDP; "attracts talent" means net migration is positive. These are descriptions, not judgments.

From classroom to workforce

Skills India's leading export sectors draw on.

  • Services (47% of exports): reading, writing and speaking well, numbers, digital skills, working with people
  • Chemicals (9% of exports): chemistry and biology (medicines, materials)
  • Minerals and energy (9% of exports): earth science, chemistry, engineering, safety

Every one of these starts with the same foundations: number sense, reading and sounds, and curiosity about the world. That is where EC Play starts, free, with Class 1 to 12.
