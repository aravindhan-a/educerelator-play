From classroom to workforce

Skills Singapore's leading export sectors draw on.

  • Services (52% of exports): reading, writing and speaking well, numbers, digital skills, working with people
  • Electronics (16% of exports): maths, physics, logic and coding
  • Machinery (11% of exports): maths, physics, engineering drawing, problem solving

Every one of these starts with the same foundations: number sense, reading and sounds, and curiosity about the world. That is where EC Play starts, free, with Primary 1.
