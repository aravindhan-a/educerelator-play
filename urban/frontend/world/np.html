From classroom to workforce

Skills Nepal's leading export sectors draw on.

  • Services (62% of exports): reading, writing and speaking well, numbers, digital skills, working with people
  • Agriculture (14% of exports): plants, soil, water and weather; measuring and recording
  • Textiles (11% of exports): design, measuring, pattern and quality, running production lines

Every one of these starts with the same foundations: number sense, reading and sounds, and curiosity about the world. That is where EC Play starts, free, with Grade 1.
