Why we exist

Most Indian ed-tech puts good practice behind a paywall and behind English. A student in a Tamil-medium or Hindi-medium classroom deserves the same quality of practice as anyone else. EC Play gives every student short, game-like practice sets that follow the school syllabus, in the language they actually think in, with no advertisements.

EC Play is completely free: no subscriptions, no paid plans, no ads. We don't measure success by revenue. We measure it by the change we help bring about in how Indian children learn, and by advocating for practice in every child's own language to become the norm, not the exception.

Who is behind this

Aravindhan Anbazhagan, Founder

Aravindhan began school in a village panchayat school in Tamil Nadu and studied in Tamil medium until Class 8, before switching to English medium. That experience shaped the core idea behind EC Play.

He is a Teach For India alumnus and has spoken at TEDx about building a contextual education system for India: the idea that learning sticks when it comes in a student's own language and context. EC Play is that idea, built.

What we promise parents

  • Completely free: no ads, no subscriptions, no paid plans
  • Child-safe: no chat, no strangers, no external links in the app
  • Minimal data: play as a guest with nothing collected, or sign in to sync progress (name, email, progress only; see our Privacy Policy)
  • Every answer independently checked by verifier scripts before a question is published
  • EC Play is independent and not affiliated with CBSE, NCERT or any school board

What EC Play covers

Class 1 to 12 across Maths, Science, English, Social Studies, Hindi, Tamil and the full Class 11–12 streams, plus revision tracks for NEET, JEE, UPSC, Olympiads and board exams. Everything works in English and 12 Indian languages: Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Urdu and Nepali.
