Astronomy

Q1. Which planet is known as the 'Red Planet'?

  1. Venus
  2. Mars
  3. Jupiter
  4. Mercury
Show answer

B. Mars — Mars is called the Red Planet because iron-oxide (rust) on its surface gives it a reddish colour.

Human Body

Q2. The number of bones in the adult human body is:

  1. 201
  2. 206
  3. 212
  4. 300
Show answer

B. 206 — An adult human has 206 bones. A newborn baby has around 300, some of which fuse together during growth.

General Science

Q3. The hardest naturally occurring substance is:

  1. Gold
  2. Iron
  3. Diamond
  4. Quartz
Show answer

C. Diamond — Diamond, a form of carbon, is the hardest naturally occurring substance (10 on the Mohs scale).

Human Body

Q4. The 'powerhouse of the cell', where energy is produced, is the:

  1. Nucleus
  2. Ribosome
  3. Mitochondrion
  4. Chloroplast
Show answer

C. Mitochondrion — The mitochondrion is called the powerhouse of the cell because it produces most of the cell's energy (ATP) through respiration.

General Science

Q5. At sea level, pure water boils at a temperature of:

  1. 50 °C
  2. 90 °C
  3. 100 °C
  4. 120 °C
Show answer

C. 100 °C — At sea level (1 atmosphere pressure), pure water boils at 100 °C and freezes at 0 °C.

General Science

Q6. The gas essential for human respiration, taken in when we breathe, is:

  1. Carbon dioxide
  2. Nitrogen
  3. Oxygen
  4. Hydrogen
Show answer

C. Oxygen — We breathe in oxygen, which cells use in respiration to release energy, and breathe out carbon dioxide as a waste product.

Astronomy

Q7. The Sun rises in the:

  1. North
  2. South
  3. East
  4. West
Show answer

C. East — Because the Earth rotates from west to east, the Sun appears to rise in the east and set in the west.

Human Body

Q8. Which organ pumps blood around the human body?

  1. Lungs
  2. Heart
  3. Brain
  4. Liver
Show answer

B. Heart — The heart is a muscular pump that circulates blood through the body. The lungs add oxygen to the blood, but the heart does the pumping.

Astronomy

Q9. The largest planet in our solar system is:

  1. Earth
  2. Saturn
  3. Jupiter
  4. Neptune
Show answer

C. Jupiter — Jupiter is the largest planet — more than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it. Saturn is the second largest.

General Science

Q10. Which of these materials is attracted by a magnet?

  1. Wood
  2. Plastic
  3. Iron
  4. Glass
Show answer

C. Iron — Iron is a magnetic material and is strongly attracted by magnets. Wood, plastic and glass are non-magnetic.

Plant Nutrition

Q11. Green plants prepare their own food using sunlight, water and carbon dioxide. What is this process called?

  1. Respiration
  2. Digestion
  3. Transpiration
  4. Photosynthesis
Show answer

D. Photosynthesis — Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make food (glucose) and release oxygen.

Air Composition

Q12. Which gas is present in the largest amount in the air around us?

  1. Nitrogen
  2. Oxygen
  3. Carbon dioxide
  4. Argon
Show answer

A. Nitrogen — Nitrogen makes up about 78% of the atmosphere, far more than oxygen (about 21%), making it the most abundant gas in air.

Simple Machines

Q13. A see-saw in a park is a common example of which simple machine?

  1. Pulley
  2. Lever
  3. Wedge
  4. Screw
Show answer

B. Lever — A see-saw turns about a fixed point (the pivot or fulcrum), which is the defining feature of a lever.

Sound Medium

Q14. Astronauts on the Moon cannot hear each other directly even when standing close. This is because sound cannot travel through a:

  1. Solid
  2. Liquid
  3. Gas
  4. Vacuum
Show answer

D. Vacuum — Sound needs a material medium (solid, liquid or gas) to travel. The Moon has almost no air, so in this near-vacuum sound cannot travel.

Vitamins Deficiency

Q15. A person whose diet lacks vitamin C is most likely to suffer from which disease?

  1. Night blindness
  2. Scurvy
  3. Rickets
  4. Goitre
Show answer

B. Scurvy — Scurvy, marked by bleeding gums and weakness, is caused by a lack of vitamin C, which is found in citrus fruits and amla.

Density Floating

Q16. A solid object placed in water will sink if its density is:

  1. Less than that of water
  2. Equal to that of water
  3. Greater than that of water
  4. Always zero
Show answer

C. Greater than that of water — An object sinks when it is denser than water; if its density is less than water it floats, and if equal it stays suspended.

Acids Bases

Q17. Blue litmus paper turns red when it is dipped into which of the following?

  1. Soap solution
  2. Lemon juice
  3. Sugar solution
  4. Common salt solution
Show answer

B. Lemon juice — Lemon juice is acidic (it contains citric acid), and acids turn blue litmus red. Soap is basic, while sugar and salt solutions are roughly neutral.

Human Blood

Q18. Which type of blood cell mainly protects the body by fighting germs and infections?

  1. Platelets
  2. Plasma
  3. White blood cells
  4. Red blood cells
Show answer

C. White blood cells — White blood cells (WBCs) defend the body against disease-causing germs. Red blood cells carry oxygen and platelets help in clotting.

Energy Resources

Q19. Which of the following is a renewable source of energy?

  1. Coal
  2. Petroleum
  3. Natural gas
  4. Solar energy
Show answer

D. Solar energy — Solar energy is renewable because sunlight is naturally replenished, whereas coal, petroleum and natural gas are fossil fuels that will run out.

Heat Conductors Insulators

Q20. You want a mug that keeps hot milk warm for the longest time. Which mug should you choose?

  1. A copper mug
  2. An aluminium mug
  3. A plastic mug
  4. An iron mug
Show answer

C. A plastic mug — Plastic is a poor conductor of heat (an insulator), so it lets heat escape slowly and keeps the milk warm longer than metal mugs, which conduct heat away quickly.

Solar System

Q21. A small body that moves around a planet in a fixed path is called a:

  1. Star
  2. Satellite
  3. Comet
  4. Asteroid
Show answer

B. Satellite — A satellite revolves around a planet; for example, the Moon is a natural satellite that revolves around Earth.

Plant Parts

Q22. Which part of a plant is mainly responsible for absorbing water and minerals from the soil?

  1. Root
  2. Leaf
  3. Flower
  4. Fruit
Show answer

A. Root — Roots have tiny root hairs that absorb water and dissolved minerals from the soil and anchor the plant.

Evaporation Cooling

Q23. Why do we feel cool when sweat evaporates from our skin on a hot day?

  1. Evaporation adds heat to the body
  2. Evaporation has no effect on temperature
  3. Evaporation absorbs heat from the body
  4. Evaporation increases body temperature
Show answer

C. Evaporation absorbs heat from the body — To change from liquid to vapour, sweat takes heat energy from the skin. This removal of heat lowers the skin's temperature, so we feel cool.

Animal Classification

Q24. Which of these animals is a mammal that lays eggs instead of giving birth to young ones?

  1. Kangaroo
  2. Whale
  3. Bat
  4. Platypus
Show answer

D. Platypus — The platypus is an egg-laying mammal (a monotreme). Kangaroos, whales and bats are all mammals that give birth to live young.

Water Cycle

Q25. During the water cycle, water vapour in the air cools and changes into tiny water droplets that form clouds. This change of state is called:

  1. Evaporation
  2. Condensation
  3. Melting
  4. Freezing
Show answer

B. Condensation — Condensation is the change from gas (water vapour) to liquid (water droplets) as it cools, which is how clouds form.
