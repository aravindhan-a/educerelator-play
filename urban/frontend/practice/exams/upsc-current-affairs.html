Polity

Q1. Which body is responsible for conducting elections in India?

  1. The Supreme Court
  2. The Election Commission of India
  3. The Reserve Bank of India
  4. The Parliament
Show answer

B. The Election Commission of India — The Election Commission of India (ECI), a permanent constitutional body under Article 324, conducts elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and the offices of the President and Vice-President.

Economy

Q2. In which year was the Reserve Bank of India established?

  1. 1935
  2. 1947
  3. 1950
  4. 1969
Show answer

A. 1935 — The RBI was established on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934. It was nationalised in 1949.

International

Q3. Where are the headquarters of the United Nations located?

  1. Geneva
  2. New York
  3. Paris
  4. Vienna
Show answer

B. New York — The UN headquarters is in New York City, USA. Geneva, Vienna and Nairobi host major UN offices, and UNESCO is headquartered in Paris.

Polity

Q4. Which body did the NITI Aayog replace in 2015?

  1. The Finance Commission
  2. The Planning Commission
  3. The Union Public Service Commission
  4. The Election Commission
Show answer

B. The Planning Commission — NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India) replaced the Planning Commission on 1 January 2015 as the government's premier policy think-tank.

Constitution

Q5. The Fundamental Duties were added to the Constitution by which amendment?

  1. 42nd Amendment
  2. 44th Amendment
  3. 1st Amendment
  4. 73rd Amendment
Show answer

A. 42nd Amendment — The Fundamental Duties (Article 51A) were added by the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976, on the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee.

Economy

Q6. In which year was the Goods and Services Tax (GST) introduced in India?

  1. 2014
  2. 2016
  3. 2017
  4. 2019
Show answer

C. 2017 — GST came into effect on 1 July 2017, subsuming most indirect taxes into a single unified tax under the 101st Constitutional Amendment.

Polity

Q7. Who appoints the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India?

  1. The Prime Minister
  2. The President
  3. The Chief Justice
  4. The Parliament
Show answer

B. The President — The CAG is appointed by the President of India under Article 148 and audits the accounts of the Union and the States.

Environment

Q8. The international agreement on climate change adopted in 2015 is known as the:

  1. Kyoto Protocol
  2. Paris Agreement
  3. Montreal Protocol
  4. Rio Declaration
Show answer

B. Paris Agreement — The Paris Agreement was adopted at COP21 in 2015 to limit global warming to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels.

Polity

Q9. The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) of India was established in which year?

  1. 1993
  2. 1985
  3. 2000
  4. 1976
Show answer

A. 1993 — The NHRC was established on 12 October 1993 under the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993.

International

Q10. The World Health Organization (WHO) is headquartered in which city?

  1. New York
  2. Geneva
  3. Rome
  4. Washington D.C.
Show answer

B. Geneva — The WHO, a specialised agency of the UN responsible for international public health, is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.

International Organisations

Q11. In which city is the headquarters of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) located?

  1. Geneva
  2. Washington, D.C.
  3. New York
  4. Paris
Show answer

B. Washington, D.C. — The IMF, one of the Bretton Woods institutions, is headquartered in Washington, D.C., close to the World Bank.

Environment Conservation

Q12. Which organisation compiles and maintains the 'Red List' of Threatened Species?

  1. World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF)
  2. United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)
  3. International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN)
  4. Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species (CITES)
Show answer

C. International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) — The IUCN Red List is the world's most comprehensive inventory of the global conservation status of species, classifying them from 'Least Concern' to 'Extinct'.

Environment Conventions

Q13. The Ramsar Convention is an international treaty for the conservation of which type of ecosystem?

  1. Wetlands
  2. Tropical forests
  3. Coral reefs
  4. Desert ecosystems
Show answer

A. Wetlands — Signed in Ramsar, Iran, the convention provides the framework for the conservation and wise use of wetlands and their resources.

International Development

Q14. How many goals are there in the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)?

  1. 8
  2. 17
  3. 15
  4. 20
Show answer

B. 17 — The SDGs, adopted in 2015 as successors to the eight Millennium Development Goals, comprise 17 goals covering issues from poverty to climate action.

Environment Conventions

Q15. The Montreal Protocol is an international agreement aimed primarily at:

  1. Reducing greenhouse gas emissions
  2. Protecting the ozone layer
  3. Conserving biological diversity
  4. Managing hazardous waste
Show answer

B. Protecting the ozone layer — The Montreal Protocol phases out the production of ozone-depleting substances such as chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) to protect the stratospheric ozone layer.

Economy Regulators

Q16. Which statutory body is responsible for regulating the securities (share) markets in India?

  1. Reserve Bank of India
  2. Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority
  3. NABARD
  4. Securities and Exchange Board of India
Show answer

D. Securities and Exchange Board of India — The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) is the regulator for the securities and commodity markets, protecting investor interests.

Polity Amendments

Q17. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act is chiefly associated with which of the following?

  1. Urban municipalities
  2. The anti-defection law
  3. Fundamental Duties
  4. Panchayati Raj institutions
Show answer

D. Panchayati Raj institutions — The 73rd Amendment (1992) gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions, adding Part IX and the Eleventh Schedule; the 74th dealt with municipalities.

Polity Parliament

Q18. Who is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha?

  1. President of India
  2. Vice President of India
  3. Prime Minister
  4. Speaker of the Lok Sabha
Show answer

B. Vice President of India — Under Article 89, the Vice President of India is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha and presides over its sittings.

International Organisations

Q19. The headquarters of the World Trade Organization (WTO) is located in which city?

  1. Geneva
  2. New York
  3. Vienna
  4. Rome
Show answer

A. Geneva — The WTO, which regulates international trade among member nations, is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.

Polity Constitutional Bodies

Q20. Under which Article of the Indian Constitution is the Finance Commission constituted?

  1. Article 148
  2. Article 356
  3. Article 280
  4. Article 370
Show answer

C. Article 280 — Article 280 provides for a Finance Commission, constituted by the President every fifth year, to recommend the distribution of taxes between the Union and States.

Polity Constitution Schedules

Q21. The Tenth Schedule of the Indian Constitution contains provisions relating to:

  1. Disqualification on grounds of defection
  2. Allocation of Rajya Sabha seats to States
  3. Languages recognised by the Constitution
  4. Forms of oaths and affirmations
Show answer

A. Disqualification on grounds of defection — The Tenth Schedule, added by the 52nd Amendment (1985), lays down the anti-defection law disqualifying members who defect from their party.

International Organisations

Q22. The Human Development Index (HDI) is published annually by which organisation?

  1. World Bank
  2. World Economic Forum
  3. International Monetary Fund
  4. United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)
Show answer

D. United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) — The UNDP publishes the HDI in its Human Development Report, combining indicators of life expectancy, education, and per-capita income.

Science Institutions

Q23. In which city is the headquarters of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) located?

  1. Sriharikota
  2. Thiruvananthapuram
  3. Bengaluru
  4. Hyderabad
Show answer

C. Bengaluru — ISRO is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka; Sriharikota hosts the launch site, while Thiruvananthapuram houses the VSSC rocket centre.

Economy Monetary Policy

Q24. In India, the 'repo rate' is determined by which of the following?

  1. Ministry of Finance
  2. Securities and Exchange Board of India
  3. NITI Aayog
  4. Reserve Bank of India
Show answer

D. Reserve Bank of India — The repo rate, at which the central bank lends to commercial banks, is set by the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee as a key monetary policy tool.

Environment Conventions

Q25. The Kyoto Protocol is primarily concerned with which of the following?

  1. Protection of the ozone layer
  2. Conservation of wetlands
  3. Control of transboundary movement of hazardous wastes
  4. Reduction of greenhouse gas emissions
Show answer

D. Reduction of greenhouse gas emissions — The Kyoto Protocol, adopted under the UNFCCC, committed industrialised nations to binding targets for reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

Economy Fiscal Policy

Q26. In the context of the Union Budget, the term 'fiscal deficit' is best defined as:

  1. The excess of revenue expenditure over revenue receipts
  2. The difference between a country's exports and imports
  3. The excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings
  4. The excess of total expenditure over total receipts including borrowings
Show answer

C. The excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings — Fiscal deficit is total government expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings; it indicates the total borrowing the government needs to meet its expenditure.
