EC Play: free practice for Class 1–12, in your students' language

Short, game-like practice sets that follow the school syllabus, in English and 12 Indian languages. Works on any phone or computer. No sign-up and no ads.

educerelator.com

Getting started (1 minute)

  1. Open educerelator.com on any phone, tablet or computer, or scan the QR code.
  2. Choose the language at the top right, then pick a class and a subject.
  3. Answer 10 questions. Each set takes about 5 minutes. No account is needed.
  4. Optional: tap Add to Home Screen to keep EC Play like an app. Questions keep working on slow connections.

In the classroom

  • 5-minute warm-up: project one set at the start of a lesson and let the class vote on each answer.
  • Exit check: end the period with a set on that day's topic to see what stuck.
  • Mother-tongue support: switch the language to Hindi, Tamil or another Indian language when a concept is new.
  • Revision weeks: use the practice pages by class and subject at educerelator.com/practice.

At home

  • One set a day: the day streak rewards a little practice every day, not long sessions.
  • Practice my mistakes: after each set, children can retry only the questions they missed.
  • Learn together: parents can play in their own language alongside their child.
  • Exam prep: revision sets for board exams, NEET, JEE, Olympiads and UPSC.

What parents and schools can count on

  • Free to play, with no advertisements
  • No chat, no strangers, no external links
  • Guest play collects no personal data
  • Every answer is checked before it is published
  • Class 1 to 12, Maths to Social Studies
  • English plus 12 Indian languages
