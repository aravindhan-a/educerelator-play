Rule used: "builds talent the world employs" means net migration is negative and remittances are at least 3% of GDP; "attracts talent" means net migration is positive. These are descriptions, not judgments.

From classroom to workforce

Skills Bangladesh's leading export sectors draw on.

  • Textiles (81% of exports): design, measuring, pattern and quality, running production lines
  • Services (14% of exports): reading, writing and speaking well, numbers, digital skills, working with people
  • Agriculture (3% of exports): plants, soil, water and weather; measuring and recording

Every one of these starts with the same foundations: number sense, reading and sounds, and curiosity about the world. That is where EC Play starts, free, with Class 1.
