From classroom to workforce

Skills United Kingdom's leading export sectors draw on.

  • Services (59% of exports): reading, writing and speaking well, numbers, digital skills, working with people
  • Machinery (10% of exports): maths, physics, engineering drawing, problem solving
  • Precious stones and metals (6% of exports): careful measuring, craft, trade and finance

Every one of these starts with the same foundations: number sense, reading and sounds, and curiosity about the world. That is where EC Play starts, free, with Year 1–2.
