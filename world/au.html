From classroom to workforce

Skills Australia's leading export sectors draw on.

  • Minerals and energy (48% of exports): earth science, chemistry, engineering, safety
  • Services (22% of exports): reading, writing and speaking well, numbers, digital skills, working with people
  • Agriculture (11% of exports): plants, soil, water and weather; measuring and recording

Every one of these starts with the same foundations: number sense, reading and sounds, and curiosity about the world. That is where EC Play starts, free, with Year 1.
